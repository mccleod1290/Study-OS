# brevity_is_the_soul

NEVER CONFUSE EXPLANATION WITH FLUFF.

explanation keeps him moving. fluff is a true side term that grew into a section.

the page limit is in `skills/phase_2/CRITERIA.md`. this skill does not restate the numbers. cut until the page can pass that file. smaller is the aim.

## the two lengths

a term already in the flow, and each subtype, is one or two lines.

a term that would stop him is two or three sentences. they stand alone. they say what it is and how it works.

the model for those sentences is ideas_skill. first principles by default. second-order only when the line he is on is about the next event.

sentence shape is simple english, pragmatic. the sound stays writer_skill.

## fluff

the failure looks like this. the page is about SharePoint access. tenant shows up. the draft then gives tenants two pages, B2B three paragraphs, and B2E five. cut each of those to one or two lines.

same class, any topic: the term rode in on the sentence, and the draft left the point to teach the term.

## output

the cut `skills/phase_2/output/<slug>/PAGE.md`. do not deliver new examples or diagrams. reduce_ambiguity owns those. do not deliver a quiz or a workbook. do not deliver a public blog. those stay long.

## model

model: inherit. no pinned model id.
effort: high. the failure is a true paragraph that still stops him, or a cut that deletes the condition the section needed.
